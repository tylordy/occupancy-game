OCCUPANCY

Your team went up to floor 8 and stopped answering the radio. You went up after them in a sealed yellow suit. The elevator is dead now, the stairwells are badge-locked, and something tall is walking the halls between the offices.

Every floor is different: offices, a hospital, a factory, a mine, a cinema maze, a prison, a mansion, and a flooding sewer at the bottom. On each one, find a keycard to unlock the stairwell down. The stairs up are always open, and unlocked stairwells stay unlocked, so you can roam between floors. Reach the street. If it sees you, break line of sight and hide in a locker, cabinet, stall, or behind a counter. When it comes close, hold your breath.

PLAY ONLINE

Up to 4 players in the same tower. The team shares one keycard, and the stairwell takes everyone together. If it grabs someone, they go down instead of dying: get to them and hold Use (E, or the USE button on a phone) to pull them back up. If the whole team goes down, the floor starts over.

Each player keeps their own shards and gear. The host's computer runs the monster, so the host should have the steadiest connection.
